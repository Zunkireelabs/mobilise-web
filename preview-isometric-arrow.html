Interaction thesis v2 — isometric brand-arrow illustration

The flat mock card gets a staged isometric backdrop: a coral ascending-steps form (computed isometric projection, hairline stroke-draw entrance, dot-stipple on the shaded face, dashed construction guides) topped by a 3D-extruded version of MOBILISE's own brand arrow — white-dominant, coral as the single accent, pure SVG + the project's existing CSS stagger, zero new dependencies.

Allowed patterns: isometric line-art (stroke-draw + dot-stipple + dashed guides — technique only, not the reference's maroon/purple palette), true 3-face extrusion on the brand arrow (lit front face + stippled side walls + dark back face, same convention as the steps), coral accent, reused arrow glyph. None else.

This page is the proposal, not the build.

Design DNA extracted from your reference (Miter onboarding flow) — measured, not eyeballed: field #6f1516, card #fefefe, CTA accent #680dbd, success #067144 (scripts/measure-colors.mjs, k=8 clustering). Technique taken forward: isometric 30° projection, flat-shaded faces (no gradients), dot-stipple on exactly one face per form, dashed construction lines off solid edges, white card floated on a bold field via contrast alone. Colors NOT carried forward — that palette is the reference brand's, not ours; MOBILISE's adaptation below swaps to coral-on-white per your direction.
